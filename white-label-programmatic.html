The model

What is white-label programmatic advertising?

White-label programmatic means a partner plans, traffics and optimizes your clients' programmatic buys — display, video, audio, streaming TV and digital out-of-home — while your agency or station sells and reports them as its own.

Most agencies don't have seats on several DSPs or the specialists to run them. Factor42 operates across DV360, The Trade Desk, Xandr, Amazon DSP and the major DOOH exchanges, with brand-safety and viewability controls on every buy.

FAQ

Common questions

What is white-label programmatic advertising?

A partner runs programmatic campaigns — display, video, audio, streaming TV and DOOH — behind the scenes, while your agency or station sells, prices and reports them under its own brand.

Which DSPs do you use?

DV360, The Trade Desk, Xandr and Amazon DSP, plus Vistar, Broadsign and Place Exchange for digital out-of-home. We manage 12+ DSPs and platforms in total.

Can you run streaming TV (OTT/CTV) campaigns?

Yes. We run connected TV on Roku, Hulu, Peacock, Amazon Fire TV and more, with household-level targeting, pre-roll, mid-roll, pause ads and interactive overlays, and cross-device attribution.

How do you keep buys brand-safe?

Every programmatic buy runs with IAS or DoubleVerify brand-safety and viewability controls, and we use PMP and guaranteed deals where the inventory calls for it.

Will my clients know you exist?

No. Every report, dashboard and client-facing deliverable carries your brand, and we never contact your clients unless you ask us to. Your team owns the relationship; we work team-to-team with you.

How much does it cost?

We charge a wholesale fulfillment rate based on your channel mix and volume, and you set the retail price for your clients. Book a consultation and we will send a rate card built around what you sell.
