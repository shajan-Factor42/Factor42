7 Questions to Vet a Provider

How to Vet a Digital Marketing Provider: 7 Questions That Reveal Everything

Choosing who runs your digital marketing is a high-stakes decision made with frustratingly little to go on. Every provider says they'll grow your business. Every one has a polished pitch and a few impressive-sounding case studies. From the outside, they can be hard to tell apart — and the difference between a good choice and an expensive mistake isn't visible in the sales deck.

The way to see past the pitch is to ask questions that are hard to fake good answers to. Here are seven that reveal what a provider is really like, and what each answer actually tells you.

1\. "How much of my money will reach customers versus cover your fees?"

What it reveals: honesty about cost. Any provider can quote a price. Far fewer will tell you plainly how much of it goes to management and overhead versus actually reaching buyers. A provider who answers this clearly is comfortable with you understanding your own costs. One who deflects is counting on you not to ask. The ease of the answer tells you how straight they'll be about money throughout the relationship.

2\. "Who specifically will run my account day to day?"

What it reveals: whether you're buying expertise or a queue. The impressive person selling you is not your campaign manager. Ask who actually does the work and what their expertise is. A good provider can tell you who'll handle your account and why they're qualified. A vague answer suggests your account gets absorbed into a system where no one really owns it.

3\. "Which channels can you run — and can you grow with me?"

What it reveals: whether they solve fragmentation or add to it. A provider who only does one channel means you'll need others to fill the gaps, recreating the multi-vendor mess. One who can run your full range of channels — and add more as you grow — can actually consolidate your marketing instead of being one more piece of the puzzle. This question separates a point solution from a real partner.

4\. "How will I see whether it's working?"

What it reveals: their relationship with accountability. Listen for whether you'll get one clear view across everything, measured honestly, or a scattered pile of platform reports you're left to interpret. Providers confident in their results make performance easy to see. Those who bury it in complexity or vague dashboards may be hiding that the results don't hold up. Transparency in reporting reflects confidence in outcomes.

5\. "Do I own my accounts and data?"

What it reveals: whether they earn your loyalty or trap it. This is the question that exposes lock-in. If your accounts, audience data, and history belong to your business, you're free to leave and they know they have to keep earning your business. If leaving means starting over, they're relying on the trap instead of the results. A provider comfortable with your ownership is confident you'll stay by choice.

6\. "What results should I realistically expect, and when?"

What it reveals: honesty versus salesmanship. The tempting answer is a bold guarantee. The trustworthy answer is a realistic one — marketing takes time, results build, and no honest provider promises overnight miracles. A provider who over-promises to win your signature will under-deliver in the relationship. Sober, specific, realistic expectations are a far better sign than a dazzling guarantee that can't be kept.

7\. "How do I leave if it's not working?"

What it reveals: their confidence in their own value. A provider who makes leaving simple is betting you won't want to. One who locks you into long terms with painful exits is protecting against the possibility that you would. The exit terms are an honest signal of how much a provider is relying on results versus contracts to keep you. Easy exit, confident provider.

The pattern across all seven

Read back over the seven and you'll notice they share a theme. None is really about marketing tactics — you can't fully judge tactics up front anyway. Every one is about \*how a provider deals with you\*: honestly or evasively, transparently or opaquely, confident in results or reliant on traps.

That's the point. The tactical quality of a provider is hard to assess before you hire them, but their character — how straight they are on money, expertise, ownership, expectations, and freedom — is highly predictive of the whole experience, and these seven questions expose it fast. A provider who answers all seven cleanly and comfortably is showing you who they'll be. So is one who squirms.
