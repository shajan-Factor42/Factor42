Why CTV Belongs in Your Small Business Media Mix in 2026

If you're planning your marketing for the year ahead and Connected TV isn't on the list, it's worth pausing to reconsider. CTV — advertising on streaming services watched on internet-connected televisions — has crossed the line from an emerging channel to one that belongs in the media mix of most small businesses. Here's the case for why it deserves a place in your plan this year.

The audience is already there — and growing

The most basic reason to be on a channel is that your customers are. On CTV, they overwhelmingly are. Viewing has shifted decisively to streaming, which now captures a larger share of TV watching than traditional cable and broadcast combined, across nearly all households and all age groups. This isn't a trend to watch and wait on — it's an established reality, and the momentum continues to build.

When an audience this large has moved to a channel, advertising follows, and the smart money gets there before the channel gets crowded and expensive. CTV in 2026 sits in a sweet spot: a huge, engaged audience, with advertising still accessible and relatively uncontested for small businesses that move now.

It's finally priced for you

The reason CTV belongs specifically in a \*small business\* mix now — as opposed to five years ago — is that it's become affordable. What was once the exclusive territory of big brands with enormous budgets is now reachable at spending levels comparable to a social media campaign. You buy by targeted audience rather than expensive broad time slots, you can start small, and production no longer costs a fortune. The economics that locked small businesses out have fundamentally changed.

That shift is what moves CTV from "someday, when we're bigger" to "this year, on our current budget." The barrier is gone; the only question is whether you take advantage before your competitors do.

It does something your other channels can't

A good media mix isn't a random collection of channels — it's a set of channels that each contribute something distinct. CTV earns its place by adding a dimension the others lack: presence and credibility on the biggest screen in the home.

Your search ads capture intent. Your social reaches people in their feeds. Your email nurtures existing relationships. CTV adds the impact and trust of television — your business on the living room screen, watched with real attention, carrying the established feel that TV uniquely conveys. It fills a gap in the mix rather than duplicating what you already do, which is exactly what a channel should do to earn a spot in your plan.

It's measurable and targeted, unlike old TV

The old objection to TV — that you couldn't target it or measure it — doesn't apply to CTV. Because it's a digital channel, it comes with digital targeting and measurement. You can aim your ads at your specific customers by location and interests, and you can measure the results rather than guessing. This is what makes CTV appropriate for a small business that needs its budget to work hard and be accountable — it combines television's impact with digital's precision.

Fitting CTV into the mix without the hassle

The key to adding CTV in 2026 is to add it as part of a coordinated mix, not as an isolated experiment. Run on its own, it's another channel to learn and manage. Run as part of a consolidated marketing operation, it slots into your existing plan seamlessly — targeted at the same customers as your other channels, measured in the same view, coordinated with everything else, and handled by one team.

That coordination is also what makes CTV perform. When your streaming ads work together with your search, social, and email — reinforcing each other across a customer's journey — the whole mix gets stronger. CTV added as a coordinated channel lifts everything; CTV bolted on in isolation is just one more disconnected effort.

Put CTV in your plan this year

Connected TV in 2026 offers a large and growing audience, small-business-friendly pricing, a distinct contribution to your mix, and real targeting and measurement. That combination is why it belongs in most small business media plans this year — not someday, now, while it's still accessible and ahead of the crowd.
