This policy explains what information Factor42 Media Inc. ("Factor42", "we", "us") collects through factor42media.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. It covers this website only. Campaign data we handle for partners is governed by our agreements with those partners, not by this policy.

Information we collect

Information you give us. When you submit the consultation form, we receive the details you enter: your name, work email, company, the type of organization you're with, the channels you select, your monthly media spend range and any message you write. The form also records the page you submitted it from.

Information collected automatically. The Site does not use analytics tools, advertising pixels or tracking cookies. Like any website, our hosting provider receives technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, and may keep it in server logs for security and reliability.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your consultation request and follow up about our services
  • To prepare proposals, rate cards and fulfillment plans you ask for
  • To keep records of our business communications
  • To protect the Site and prevent spam and abuse

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run the Site and deliver form submissions:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the Site and processes visit data as described above.
  • Web3Forms receives consultation form submissions and delivers them to our email.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we receive and send.

These providers process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies. Links to other sites, such as our LinkedIn page, are governed by those sites' policies.

How long we keep information

We keep consultation requests and related correspondence for as long as needed to respond to you and to maintain reasonable business records, and then delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information you've sent us, or to stop contacting you. Depending on where you live, including California and the European Economic Area, you may have additional rights under local law. We will respond to verified requests as the law requires and will not treat you differently for making one.

Security

The Site is served over encrypted HTTPS connections. We take reasonable steps to protect the information you send us, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

The Site is intended for businesses and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
