The model

What is white-label PPC?

White-label PPC (pay-per-click) means a specialist team runs your clients' paid search and paid social campaigns while everything the client sees — dashboards, reports, emails — is branded as your agency. You sell the service at your price, pay a wholesale fulfillment rate, and keep the difference.

It's the fastest way to say yes to more PPC clients without hiring, training and licensing tools for an in-house team. Factor42 handles campaign builds, bidding, negative keywords, audience layering, creative rotation, pacing and reporting; you stay the face of the account.

FAQ

Common questions

What is white-label PPC management?

A specialist partner builds, optimizes and reports on your clients' pay-per-click campaigns under your agency's brand. Your client works with you; the partner works behind the scenes.

Will my clients know you exist?

No. Every report, dashboard and client-facing deliverable carries your brand, and we never contact your clients unless you ask us to. Your team owns the relationship; we work team-to-team with you.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. Wherever the platform allows, we work inside the accounts you own and grant us access to, so you can see who has access and remove it at any time. If the partnership ends, everything stays with you.

How much does it cost?

We charge a wholesale fulfillment rate based on your channel mix and volume, and you set the retail price for your clients. Book a consultation and we will send a rate card built around what you sell.

How fast can a campaign launch?

Trafficking turnaround averages 12 minutes, and Google Ads campaigns launch in under four hours on average once the brief and creative are in.

Which PPC platforms do you manage?

Google Ads (Search, Display, Performance Max, Shopping), YouTube, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), TikTok, LinkedIn and Reddit, with Google Analytics 4 and Campaign Manager 360 integration.
