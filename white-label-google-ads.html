The model

How white-label Google Ads works

Your agency sells Google Ads management to the client. Factor42 does the hands-on work inside the client's Google Ads account, which you own and grant us access to: account structure, keyword research, campaign builds, bidding, negative keywords and ongoing optimization.

Reporting goes out with your branding, through Google Analytics 4 and Campaign Manager 360 where you use them. Your account managers present the results; we keep the account tuned between meetings.

FAQ

Common questions

What is white-label Google Ads management?

A partner manages your clients' Google Ads campaigns behind the scenes while your agency sells, owns and presents the service. Factor42 builds, optimizes and reports; your client only ever sees your brand.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. Wherever the platform allows, we work inside the accounts you own and grant us access to, so you can see who has access and remove it at any time. If the partnership ends, everything stays with you.

Will my clients know you exist?

No. Every report, dashboard and client-facing deliverable carries your brand, and we never contact your clients unless you ask us to. Your team owns the relationship; we work team-to-team with you.

How fast can a campaign launch?

Trafficking turnaround averages 12 minutes, and Google Ads campaigns launch in under four hours on average once the brief and creative are in.

How much does it cost?

We charge a wholesale fulfillment rate based on your channel mix and volume, and you set the retail price for your clients. Book a consultation and we will send a rate card built around what you sell.

Do you manage YouTube and Performance Max too?

Yes. Alongside Search, Shopping and Display, we run YouTube in-stream, bumper, non-skippable and Masthead campaigns, and Performance Max with brand and budget guardrails.
