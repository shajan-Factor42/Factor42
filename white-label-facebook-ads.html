The model

What white-label Facebook ads management covers

White-label Facebook ads management means a specialist team builds and optimizes your clients' Meta campaigns inside the Business Manager accounts you control, and every report carries your agency's brand. You set the price; we do the work.

Paid social changes fast — formats, placements, audiences and creative fatigue all move week to week. A dedicated team keeps campaigns current, so your account managers can focus on the client instead of the ads manager.

FAQ

Common questions

What is white-label Facebook ads management?

A partner runs your clients' Facebook and Instagram ad campaigns behind the scenes while your agency sells and presents the service under its own brand.

Who owns the ad accounts?

You do. Wherever the platform allows, we work inside the accounts you own and grant us access to, so you can see who has access and remove it at any time. If the partnership ends, everything stays with you.

Will my clients know you exist?

No. Every report, dashboard and client-facing deliverable carries your brand, and we never contact your clients unless you ask us to. Your team owns the relationship; we work team-to-team with you.

How much does it cost?

We charge a wholesale fulfillment rate based on your channel mix and volume, and you set the retail price for your clients. Book a consultation and we will send a rate card built around what you sell.

Do you also run TikTok, LinkedIn and Reddit ads?

Yes. The same team runs TikTok in-feed video, LinkedIn Sponsored Content and Reddit campaigns, with lookalike, retargeting and custom audiences and creative testing on each.

Do you create the ad creative?

We QA every creative asset against platform specs and run the A/B tests. Creative production is scoped separately; tell us what you need in your consultation.
