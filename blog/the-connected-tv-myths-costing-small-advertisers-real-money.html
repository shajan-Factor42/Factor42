Blog/Channels & platforms

Connected TV — advertising on streaming services watched on internet-connected televisions — is one of the biggest opportunities in marketing right now, and one of the most misunderstood. A set of stubborn myths keeps small businesses away from it, and those myths are expensive, because they keep businesses out of a channel that has quietly become both affordable and effective for exactly their size.

Factor42 Media4 min read

Let's clear away the myths one by one, because each is costing small advertisers real money in missed opportunity.

Myth 1: "TV advertising is only for big brands"

This is the big one, and it's badly out of date. It was true in the era of traditional broadcast TV, when advertising meant huge production budgets and expensive network time slots that only national brands could afford.

Connected TV works completely differently. You can run a genuine streaming TV campaign on a modest budget — in the same range as a social media campaign — because you buy by targeted audience rather than expensive broad time slots, and modern tools have brought production costs down to earth. The barrier that kept small businesses off television for decades is simply gone. Believing otherwise means leaving the living-room screen entirely to competitors who figured this out.

Myth 2: "My customers aren't watching streaming"

Whatever your customer base, the odds are overwhelming that they've moved to streaming, because nearly everyone has. Streaming now captures a larger share of TV viewing than traditional cable and broadcast combined, and the vast majority of households own internet-connected TV devices. This isn't a young-person phenomenon — audiences across age groups have shifted their viewing to streaming.

Assuming your customers aren't there is assuming they're the rare exception to a nearly universal shift. They're watching. The only question is whether your business shows up while they do.

Myth 3: "You can't target or measure TV ads"

Old TV advertising was a blunt instrument — you paid to reach everyone watching a show and hoped the right people were among them, with little ability to measure the result. This myth carries that reputation over to Connected TV, where it no longer applies.

CTV is a digital channel, which means it comes with digital targeting and measurement. You can aim your ads at specific audiences — by location, interests, and more — so your budget reaches the people who might actually buy, not a random broadcast crowd. And you can measure the results rather than guessing. CTV combines the impact of television with the precision of digital advertising, which is exactly what makes it so valuable for small businesses that can't afford to waste budget.

Myth 4: "It's too complicated to bother with"

The final myth is that CTV is a technical hassle to set up and run — another complex channel to learn on top of everything else. On your own, there'd be some truth to that; any channel takes know-how to run well.

But this is precisely where running CTV as part of a consolidated marketing operation removes the barrier entirely. When streaming TV is one channel in a unified plan run by one team, there's no new platform for you to master, no separate vendor to manage, no technical setup on your plate. It's handled — targeted at the same customers as your other channels, measured in the same clear view, coordinated with the rest of your marketing. The complexity myth only holds if you're going it alone.

The real cost of believing the myths

Add these myths up and you see the true price of them: they keep small businesses out of a channel that is affordable, that reaches nearly all their customers, that offers real targeting and measurement, and that can be run without hassle as part of a consolidated plan. Every month those myths go unchallenged is a month competitors get to own the living-room screen unopposed — often before the local market gets crowded.

The businesses that move early on CTV, while the myths keep others away, tend to capture attention that's still relatively uncontested and increasingly affordable. The myths aren't just wrong; they're a head start you're handing to whoever ignores them first.

Don't let myths make your decisions

Connected TV is no longer a big-brand luxury, a young-person channel, an untargetable guess, or a technical nightmare. It's an affordable, precise, measurable way for a small business to put itself on the biggest screen in the customer's home — especially when run as part of a coordinated marketing plan.
