Blog/Choosing a partner

If you run a local business, you've probably heard the anxious talk: artificial intelligence is upending search, AI is answering questions directly, and the old rules of getting found online are crumbling. It's enough to make a small business owner wonder whether local SEO is even worth the effort anymore.

Factor42 Media4 min read

Here's the reassuring reality. AI is genuinely changing search — but local search, the kind that matters most to a business serving a specific area, is among the most resilient corners of the whole landscape. Understanding why will help you focus your effort where it still pays off handsomely, instead of panicking over changes that don't affect you as much as the headlines suggest.

What AI is actually changing

The big shift is that AI-powered search increasingly answers questions directly. Ask a broad informational question and you may get a generated answer at the top of the page, sometimes without needing to click through to any website at all. For certain kinds of content — general how-to and informational queries — this genuinely changes how people find answers, and businesses that relied on ranking for those broad questions are feeling it.

That's the disruption driving the anxious headlines, and for some types of businesses and content, it's real and worth adapting to. But notice the specific kind of query where AI answers dominate: broad, informational, general-knowledge questions. That's not how people search when they're looking for a local business.

Why local search is more resilient

When someone needs a local business, they don't search the way they search for general information. They search with local, transactional intent: the plumber near them, a dentist accepting new patients in their town, the best place nearby for what they need right now. These searches are fundamentally different, and they've been far less disrupted by AI answers.

The reason is simple. AI is good at answering general questions, but "which specific local business should I actually go to, and how do I reach it?" isn't a general question — it's a local, practical one that still leads people to maps, listings, reviews, and business websites. When someone's ready to call, visit, or buy from a business near them, they still need the concrete details that local search provides. That behavior has stayed remarkably stable even as broader search evolves. For local businesses, the ground is far steadier than the headlines suggest.

What still matters for getting found locally

Because local search remains resilient, the fundamentals of local SEO still pay off — arguably more than ever, since some competitors may wrongly neglect them out of AI panic. The things that help customers find a local business still work:

  • A strong, complete business listing — accurate name, address, hours, and details, so you show up when people search nearby and can act on what they find.
  • Reviews — they influence both whether you appear in local results and whether customers choose you once they see you. They remain powerful.
  • Local relevance on your website — clearly signaling where you are and what you do, so search engines and customers alike understand you serve their area.
  • Consistency across the web — the same accurate business information everywhere it appears, which builds the trust that local search rewards.
  • None of these have been made obsolete by AI. If anything, doing them well is a reliable advantage precisely while others are distracted.

Don't panic — position

The smart response to AI's effect on search isn't to abandon local SEO; it's to keep doing the fundamentals well while staying aware of the broader shift. A local business that maintains a strong listing, gathers reviews, and keeps its information consistent will continue to be found by the customers who matter — because those customers are searching in exactly the resilient, local, ready-to-act way that AI has changed the least.

There's also an opportunity in the anxiety. As some businesses neglect their local SEO out of confusion about AI, the ones that stay disciplined stand out more. The panic is a chance to gain ground, not lose it.

Stay found where it counts

AI is reshaping parts of search, but local search — the kind that brings customers to a business serving their area — remains one of the most stable and valuable channels a local business has. The fundamentals still work, the customers are still searching, and the businesses that keep showing up will keep getting found.
