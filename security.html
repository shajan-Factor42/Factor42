Security & confidentiality

As a white-label partner, we work inside your ad accounts and see your clients' campaigns. Protecting that access, and keeping your client relationships confidential, is part of the job.

Your accounts stay yours

We work in the ad platforms, ad servers and data tools you grant us access to. Wherever the platform allows, access is given to named team members through your own account permissions, so you can see who has access and remove it at any time. When a partnership ends, we hand back everything we manage and our access is removed.

Access on a need-to-know basis

Only the people working on your campaigns have access to your accounts and data. Access is limited to what their role requires and reviewed when people change roles or leave.

Confidentiality

Your client list, pricing, campaign data and performance are confidential. Our commitments are set out in your master services agreement, and we're glad to sign a mutual NDA before any detailed conversation. We never contact your clients unless you ask us to, and we never use your data for anyone else's campaigns.

How we handle campaign data

We use your data only to deliver, optimize and report on your campaigns. We don't sell it, and we don't combine it with other partners' data. Reports and files are shared through the channels you choose.

This website

factor42media.com is served over encrypted HTTPS and doesn't use tracking cookies or analytics. See our privacy policy for details.

Security questions and reports

If you have a security questionnaire for your vendor review, or you believe you've found a security issue with our site or services, contact us and we'll respond promptly.
